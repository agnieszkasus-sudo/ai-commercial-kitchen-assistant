# AI Assistant for Commercial Kitchen & Ventilation Hygiene

**Building AI course project**

## Summary

This project is about creating an AI assistant for commercial kitchen and ventilation hygiene companies. The idea is to reduce administrative work by helping with scheduling, documentation, reports and quality checks. AI would support managers in their daily work and help improve efficiency.

---

## Background

I work in commercial kitchen and ventilation hygiene, where every project requires careful planning, documentation and communication with clients.

Every day we prepare work schedules, RAMS, COSHH assessments, quotations, reports and invoices. We also review hundreds of before and after photos from different sites. These tasks take a lot of time and are mostly done manually.

This project was inspired by my everyday work. I believe AI could support these processes and allow managers to spend more time on customers and less time on administration.

---

## How is it used?

The system would be used by operations managers, project managers and team leaders.

The AI assistant could help to:

- organise work schedules
- suggest the best team allocation
- prepare RAMS and COSHH documents
- check reports before sending them to clients
- review before and after photos
- remind managers about missing documents
- help prepare quotations and invoices

The assistant would not replace people. It would simply make everyday work faster and more organised.

---

## Data sources and AI methods

The project could use:

- cleaning reports
- before and after photographs
- RAMS documents
- COSHH assessments
- site specifications
- work schedules
- historical project information

Possible AI methods include:

- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Computer Vision for image analysis
- Machine Learning
- Large Language Models (LLMs)

---

## Challenges

AI cannot replace experience or human judgement.

Cleaning quality should always be checked by trained operatives and managers. The system also needs good quality data and must protect confidential client information.

---

## What next?

In the future I would like the assistant to:

- automatically analyse cleaning photographs
- create first drafts of reports
- integrate with Microsoft 365
- help plan work across multiple teams
- predict how long a job will take based on previous projects

My goal is to build a practical tool that helps commercial cleaning companies save time while maintaining high standards.
